Ingredient spotlight

Three things worth buying good versions of.

  • Olive Oil

    Finish with it, don't just cook with it — a good pour at the end is half the flavor.

  • Fresh Herbs

    Added last, torn not chopped. Dried herbs go in early; fresh ones go in right before serving.

  • Flaky Salt

    Season in layers while you cook, then finish with a pinch of something flaky for texture.

Cook's notes

  • On timing

    Read the whole recipe before you start. Twice. Most kitchen disasters are just bad sequencing.|

  • On substitutions

    Swap vegetables freely. Don't swap acid, fat, or salt — those are what actually hold a dish together.|

  • On leftovers

    Curries and stews are always better the next day. Cook them a day ahead on purpose, not by accident.|
