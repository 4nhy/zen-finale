Chicken Piccata

Thin-pounded cutlets, dredged and seared until the crust turns gold, finished in a bright, buttery pan sauce of lemon, capers, and white wine.

35 min totalPrep 10 minCook 25 minServes 4Medium

Ingredients

Method

  1. 1

    Season the cutlets with salt and pepper, then dredge in flour, shaking off the excess.

  2. 2

    Heat 2 tbsp olive oil and 2 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the cutlets in batches, 2–3 minutes per side, until golden. Set aside.

  3. 3

    Add the remaining oil to the pan, pour in the wine, and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer 2 minutes to cook off the alcohol.

  4. 4

    Add the stock, capers, and lemon juice. Simmer 4–5 minutes until slightly reduced.

  5. 5

    Off the heat, whisk in the remaining 2 tbsp butter until the sauce turns glossy.

  6. 6

    Return the chicken to the pan, spoon the sauce over, and simmer 2 minutes to warm through. Finish with parsley.

Cook's note

Pound the cutlets to an even thickness — uneven pieces mean thin edges overcook before the center is done.
