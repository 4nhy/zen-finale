About

Built by people tired of bad job boards.

Three of us were job-hunting at the same time in 2024, comparing notes on how much of every board was noise — reposted listings, roles that had been filled for weeks, descriptions written by someone who'd never spoken to the hiring manager.

We started keeping a shared spreadsheet of roles we'd actually called and confirmed were open. Friends asked to see it. Then friends of friends. The Roster is that spreadsheet, grown up — still built on the same rule: nothing goes on the board until someone here has confirmed it's real.

01 — What we hold ourselves to

Verify, then list.

Every role is confirmed with the hiring manager before it goes on the board — not scraped from a company's careers page and left to rot.

Delist on day one.

A filled role comes down the day it's filled, not whenever someone gets around to it.

No side deals.

We don't take payment to rank a listing higher. Order is category, then recency. That's it.

Write it straight.

Descriptions come from whoever's actually hiring, not a template that says "fast-paced environment."

02 — How we got here

Jan 2024

The spreadsheet

Three of us, job-hunting at the same time, start tracking roles we've personally called and confirmed are still open.

Jun 2024

Friends of friends

The sheet gets shared past our own circle. It crosses 200 rows and starts breaking in Google Sheets.

Nov 2024

The Roster, v1

A real site replaces the spreadsheet — same verification rule, now with actual category and location filtering.

Mar 2025

The verification pipeline

Every listing gets a confirmation call before it goes live, and comes down automatically the day a company tells us it's filled.

2026

Today

Dozens of companies, hundreds of roles verified since launch, and still zero recruiter reposts.
