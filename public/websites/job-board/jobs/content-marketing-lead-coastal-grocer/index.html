Content Marketing Lead

Coastal Grocer · Chicago, IL · Full-time · Mid

Coastal Grocer runs 14 regional grocery stores. You'd write and plan the content that gets someone into a specific store on a specific Saturday.

Coastal Grocer operates 14 grocery stores across the Chicago area, each with its own regulars, its own weekly specials, and its own slightly different reason people shop there. Corporate marketing has historically been one generic email blast a week to everyone, regardless of which store someone actually shops at — open rates have been sliding for two years.

You'd lead a rebuild of that approach: store-level content (a produce manager talking about what's in season locally, a weekend recipe tied to an actual in-store promotion) distributed through email and our underused Instagram accounts, coordinated with a part-time copywriter and a freelance photographer we already work with. You'd set the calendar, write or edit most of the copy yourself, and report on what's actually moving foot traffic versus what just gets opened.

This role reports to the VP of Marketing and works closely with individual store managers, several of whom have been skeptical of past marketing initiatives that didn't account for how different their stores actually are. Part of the job is earning that trust store by store.

What you'll need

  • 4+ years in content or lifecycle marketing, ideally for a multi-location retail or hospitality business
  • Strong, clear writing — you'll be asked for a writing sample as part of the process, not just a portfolio link
  • Experience with email platforms (we use Klaviyo) and basic performance reporting
  • Comfortable visiting stores regularly and working directly with non-marketing staff who have limited time
  • Photography or basic video skills are a plus given the freelance budget is limited
