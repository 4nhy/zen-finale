Marketing & Community Intern

Coastal Grocer · Chicago, IL · Internship · Internship

A part-time, school-year internship spent in actual stores, helping build the store-level content program from the ground up.

This is a part-time internship (roughly 15 hours/week during the school year) supporting Coastal Grocer's new store-level content effort. You'd rotate between 2–3 stores, interviewing produce managers and staff, taking photos, and drafting short pieces of content — a seasonal-produce writeup, a staff pick, a short recipe tied to a weekly promotion — under the direction of our Content Marketing Lead.

Unlike a lot of marketing internships, this one is not primarily a desk job. You'd be expected to physically go to stores, talk to people who aren't marketers, and bring back material that's actually specific to that location rather than generic. You'd also get exposure to how the content performs once it's published — open rates, engagement, and what store managers say afterward.

This role is a good fit for someone who wants to build a real portfolio of published, results-tracked work rather than intern-tier busywork.

What you'll need

  • Currently pursuing a degree in marketing, communications, journalism, or a related field
  • Comfortable writing short-form content on a deadline and taking direction on tone and edits
  • Able to travel between Chicago-area store locations (mileage reimbursed) roughly twice a week
  • Basic photography skills (a phone camera is fine) — no professional equipment required
  • Available for the fall or spring semester, 15 hours/week, with flexible scheduling around classes
