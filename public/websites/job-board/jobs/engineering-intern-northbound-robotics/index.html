Engineering Intern, Summer 2027

Northbound Robotics · Pittsburgh, PA · Internship · Internship

A 12-week internship on the Platform team, working on the same fleet-management tools the full-time engineers ship to production.

This is a 12-week summer internship on Northbound's Platform team, the group that keeps roughly 900 warehouse robots updated and monitored in the field. You won't be doing a side project disconnected from the real product — interns here get a scoped piece of an actual roadmap item, this year likely related to improving the diagnostic tooling field technicians use when a robot goes offline.

You'd be paired with a full-time engineer as a mentor, attend the same team standups as everyone else, and present your work at the company-wide intern demo day in week 11. Past interns have shipped things that stayed in production — one built a dashboard view that's still how the ops team checks fleet-wide battery health.

We keep our intern cohort small (2–3 people) on purpose, so you'll get real one-on-one time with engineers rather than being one of thirty interns competing for attention.

What you'll need

  • Currently pursuing a degree in computer science, computer engineering, or a related field, graduating December 2027 or later
  • Comfortable with at least one of Python, Go, or C++ from coursework or personal projects
  • Curiosity about systems that touch the physical world — you don't need robotics experience, just genuine interest
  • Able to work in-office in Pittsburgh for the full 12 weeks; we don't offer a remote option for this program
  • A GitHub profile, class project, or personal project you can walk us through is more useful to us than a GPA
