Design Intern, Summer 2027

Fieldstone · Portland, OR · Internship · Internship

A 10-week internship helping design the store-visit research program, then turning what we learn into real interface changes.

Fieldstone's design team is two people, and this internship is deliberately structured to give you exposure to the full loop — research, design, and seeing a change ship — rather than one narrow slice of it. You'd join our monthly in-store research visits (Pacific Northwest hardware and farm-supply stores), take notes and photos, and help synthesize what we saw into concrete design problems.

From there, you'd take ownership of at least one small, real interface improvement from sketch through a shipped version reviewed by actual users on a following store visit. Past interns have redesigned parts of the receiving flow's confirmation screens and a low-stock alert that store staff said they kept accidentally dismissing.

This internship includes travel to 2–3 stores over the summer (expenses covered) as a core part of the program, not an occasional extra.

What you'll need

  • Currently pursuing a degree in design, HCI, or a related field, or equivalent self-directed portfolio work
  • Some prior exposure to Figma and basic usability research methods (a class project counts)
  • Comfortable observing and talking to people who are not designers and don't think in design terms
  • Able to work from our Portland studio for the 10-week program, with occasional regional travel
  • A portfolio or writing sample showing how you think through a problem, not just polished final screens
