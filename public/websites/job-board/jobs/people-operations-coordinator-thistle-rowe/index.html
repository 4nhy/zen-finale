People Operations Coordinator

Thistle & Rowe · Austin, TX · Full-time · Junior

Thistle & Rowe just crossed 80 employees, which is exactly when the informal way of doing HR stops working. You'd help build the formal version.

Thistle & Rowe designs and manufactures home goods, and has grown from 20 to 80 people over the past two years without much dedicated People function — onboarding has been a shared spreadsheet, benefits questions go to whoever answers Slack first, and there's no consistent process for something as basic as a new hire's first-week checklist.

You'd be the first dedicated People Ops hire, reporting to the Head of Operations, and would start by simply documenting what currently happens (inconsistently) so it can be made consistent — onboarding, benefits enrollment, the PTO request process, and basic employee-records hygiene. This is a builder role: a lot of what you'll do doesn't exist yet as a defined process.

You'd work closely with a PEO (professional employer organization) that handles payroll and benefits administration, so this isn't a from-scratch HR build, but you would be the internal point of contact translating between the PEO's systems and what actually makes sense for an 80-person manufacturing and design company.

What you'll need

  • 1–2 years in an HR, people operations, or office/business operations role
  • Comfortable creating process documentation from scratch — this role has more ambiguity than a typical HR coordinator job at a larger company
  • Organized and detail-oriented enough to manage employee records accurately without a mature HRIS doing it for you
  • Experience working with a PEO (we use Justworks) is a plus but not required
  • Based in or willing to relocate to Austin — this is an in-office role at our design studio
