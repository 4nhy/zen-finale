Operations Manager

Mercer Freight · Newark, NJ · Full-time · Senior

Mercer coordinates freight for about 30 regional trucking partners. You'd run the daily dispatch floor where the plan meets what actually happens.

Mercer Freight is a freight brokerage matching shippers with a network of about 30 regional trucking companies across the Northeast. The job of the operations floor is simple to describe and hard to do well: a load has to be picked up and delivered on time, and when a truck breaks down, a driver runs out of hours, or a shipper's dock closes early, someone has to re-solve the problem in real time, usually on the phone.

You'd manage a team of 5 dispatchers, own the daily standup that reviews at-risk loads, and be the escalation point when a dispatcher can't resolve something themselves — a carrier no-show two hours before a pickup, a rate dispute, a shipment that's going to miss a delivery window no matter what. You'd also own the monthly report to leadership on on-time performance and where it's slipping.

This is an in-person, floor-based role — the team works from our Newark office and the job is largely about being physically present when things go sideways, not managing from a dashboard remotely.

What you'll need

  • 4+ years in freight, logistics, or supply chain operations, with at least 1 year in a people-management role
  • Comfortable making a fast decision with incomplete information and owning the outcome
  • Experience with a TMS (transportation management system) — we use McLeod
  • Direct, calm communication style; dispatchers and carriers both need clear instructions under pressure, not vague ones
  • Available for occasional early-morning or evening escalations during peak shipping weeks
