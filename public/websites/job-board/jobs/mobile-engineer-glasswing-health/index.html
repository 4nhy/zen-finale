Mobile Engineer, iOS

Glasswing Health · Boston, MA · Full-time · Senior

Glasswing's app is how about 40,000 people track a chronic condition daily. Reliability isn't a nice-to-have here — a missed reminder has consequences.

Glasswing Health builds a companion app for people managing chronic kidney disease — medication reminders, lab result tracking, and a symptom log that patients share with their care team. It's a quiet, unglamorous kind of app: no feed, no engagement loops, just something that has to work correctly every day for people who are often already dealing with a lot.

You'd be the second dedicated iOS engineer, working alongside one Android engineer and a shared backend team. Current focus is moving our notification scheduling off a brittle local-notifications approach (which silently fails after iOS updates more often than we'd like) onto a more resilient server-driven push architecture — while keeping offline logging fully functional, since patients often complete their symptom log without signal.

We run a limited clinical pilot with two nephrology practices, so occasionally a bug report comes with a patient's actual medical context attached, redacted but real. That changes how carefully you want to test things before they ship.

What you'll need

  • 5+ years of iOS development, Swift and SwiftUI, with at least one app you shipped that's still in the App Store
  • Experience with offline-first data patterns — local persistence, sync conflict resolution, retry logic
  • Comfortable with notification scheduling, background tasks, and the specific pain of iOS's changing rules around both
  • Health/regulated-industry experience is a plus but not required; what matters more is caring about correctness under real constraints
  • HIPAA-adjacent work requires a background check as part of the offer process
