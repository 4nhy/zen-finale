Platform Engineer

Northbound Robotics · Pittsburgh, PA · Full-time · Mid

Our warehouse robots ship firmware over the air to about 900 units in the field. You'd build the pipeline that gets it there safely.

Northbound builds autonomous pallet-moving robots for mid-size distribution warehouses. Each robot runs a Linux-based stack on an embedded board, and every unit we've shipped since 2023 phones home to a fleet-management service that tracks battery health, navigation errors, and firmware version.

The Platform team (4 engineers) owns that fleet service, the OTA update pipeline, and the internal tooling our field techs use to diagnose a robot that's gotten stuck or gone dark. Right now, rolling out a firmware update to the full fleet takes about six hours of staged rollout and manual monitoring — your first project would likely be automating the rollback trigger so a bad update halts itself instead of a human noticing three warehouses' worth of error logs at 11pm.

You'd work closely with the two firmware engineers and occasionally with the warehouse ops team when something goes wrong on-site. About once a quarter someone from Platform travels to a customer site to debug a fleet-wide issue in person — that's not required, but people who've done it say it's the part of the job that changes how they write the fleet code.

What you'll need

  • 3+ years working on backend or infrastructure systems, ideally something with a physical or embedded component
  • Comfortable with Python or Go for service code, and enough Linux systems knowledge to read a kernel panic and not panic yourself
  • Experience with a message queue or pub/sub system (we use MQTT for device comms, Kafka internally)
  • Have shipped something where a bad deploy had real-world consequences beyond a dashboard error
  • No robotics background required — most of the team came from web infrastructure
