04 — Why The Roster

Not a job aggregator.

Every listing is real.

Roles are verified within 48 hours of posting and delisted the day they're filled — no ghost postings padding the count.

Filter by what matters.

Category, location, type, level. No keyword soup, no "relevance" black box.

No recruiter-speak.

Descriptions come from the hiring manager, not a template — you'll know what the job actually is before you apply.

Direct, every time.

Apply straight to the company. No account wall, no résumé black hole.
